Our Story

Serving Mumbai with Quality & Trust

Memon Cloth Store has been a cornerstone of the Kalyan community for years, providing families with high-quality clothing and fabrics at prices that respect their budgets. Located on Ghass Bazar Road, our store has grown from a small shop to a trusted name in clothing retail.

Founded with a simple belief — that everyone deserves access to quality fabrics — we have built lasting relationships with our customers through honest dealings, premium products, and a warm, welcoming shopping experience.

From Dresses that withstand daily wear to elegant fabrics for special occasions, every product in our store is carefully selected to meet our high standards of quality and value.

Our Mission

To provide every customer with access to premium quality clothing and fabrics at affordable prices, while maintaining the highest standards of customer service and community trust.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and preferred clothing store in Mumbai, known for our unwavering commitment to quality, affordability, and customer satisfaction across every interaction.
