Return & Refund Policy

Our commitment to your satisfaction

Home / Return Policy

7-Day Replace / Refund Window

We want you to be completely happy with your purchase. If something isn't right, you can request a replacement or refund within 7 days of your order being marked "Delivered."

How to Request a Return

1. Go to My Orders on your account and find the delivered order.

2. Click "Request Replace / Refund" and choose whether you'd like a replacement or a refund, and tell us the reason.

3. Our team will review your request and update you on its status (Approved, Rejected, or Completed) through the same order page.

Conditions for Return

  • Requests must be raised within 7 days of delivery — requests after this window cannot be accepted.
  • Items must be unused, unwashed, and in their original condition with tags intact.
  • Please keep the product ready for pickup/exchange once your request is approved.

Refunds

Once a refund request is approved and the returned item is received in good condition, refunds are processed to your original payment method. For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds will be made via bank transfer or UPI — our team will contact you to arrange this.

Reach out to us on WhatsApp at +91 84528 03023 or visit us at our Kalyan store, and we'll be happy to assist.

Shipping Policy

How we get your order to you

Home / Shipping Policy

Delivery Charges

We're currently offering free delivery on all orders for a limited introductory period. Standard delivery charges will apply after this period — any applicable charge will always be clearly shown at checkout before you place your order.

Dispatch & Delivery Timeline

Orders are usually dispatched within 48 hours of being placed.

Standard delivery within India takes 7 to 8 business days from the dispatch date, depending on your location. Please note that Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays are not considered working days for delivery timelines. You'll receive updates on your order status (Packed, Shipped, Out for Delivery, Delivered) in the "My Orders" section of your account, along with an email confirmation when you place your order.

Cash on Delivery (COD)

Cash on Delivery is available for most locations. Please note a small additional charge may apply for COD orders — this will be shown at checkout before you confirm your order.

Order Tracking

You can track your order's progress anytime by logging in and visiting "My Orders."

Delayed Orders

If your order hasn't been dispatched within the estimated timeframe, please reach out to us and our team will look into it right away.

Questions?

How we handle your information

Home / Privacy Policy

Memon Cloth Store ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you create an account or place an order, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google. Payment details are processed securely by Razorpay — we do not store your card or bank information.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To send order confirmations and delivery updates via email or WhatsApp
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support

Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We share order details only with delivery partners (to fulfill your order) and payment processors (to process payments) as necessary to complete your purchase.

Our Story

Serving Mumbai with Quality & Trust

Memon Cloth Store has proudly served the Kalyan community for decades, carrying forward a legacy of trust, quality, and customer satisfaction. While our retail store was established in 1999, our family's journey in the cloth business began in 1970, giving us over five decades of experience in the textile and clothing industry.

Built on the values of honesty, quality, and affordability, Memon Cloth Store has become a trusted destination for families looking for premium fabrics, ready-made garments, dress materials, and school uniforms. We carefully select every product to ensure our customers receive the best combination of quality, style, and value.

Located near Memon Masjid, Kalyan, we continue our tradition of providing a warm and personalized shopping experience. Whether you're shopping for everyday wear, festive occasions, or premium fabrics, our experienced team is committed to helping you find the perfect choice for every need.

To provide every customer with access to premium quality clothing and fabrics at affordable prices, while maintaining the highest standards of customer service and community trust.
